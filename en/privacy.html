1. Overview

Your privacy matters a great deal to us, and we designed Fawran Tools from the ground up so that most file processing happens locally, right in your browser, without needing to upload anything to a server. This page explains in detail what data we may collect, why, and how we use it, so you're fully informed before using any of our tools.

2. File & image processing

All Fawran Tools utilities that handle images or files (such as the WebP converter, image compressor, and PDF merge/split tools) process those files locally inside your browser using standard web technologies (such as JavaScript and WebAssembly). We do not upload your images or files to our servers and we never keep a copy of them — processing happens entirely on your device, and the file is cleared from browser memory once you close or refresh the page.

The only exception is tools that explicitly rely on an external service to perform their function (such as a website speed check, or fetching YouTube video data) — in that case, the link you enter (not a personal file) is what gets sent to that external service, as explained in section 6 below.

3. Data we may collect

We may collect general, non-personal usage data using analytics tools such as Google Analytics, in order to understand how the site is used and to improve it. Examples of this data include:

Data typeExample
Browser and device informationBrowser type, operating system, screen size
Browsing behaviorMost visited pages, approximate visit duration, referral source
Approximate geographic locationCountry or city (derived from IP address, not a precise location)

This data is aggregated and statistical in nature, and we do not use it to identify you personally.

4. Local storage in your browser

The site uses your browser's built-in local storage feature to save certain preferences, including:

  • Your light/dark mode preference for the site.
  • Your list of "favorite" or "recently used" tools, where that feature is available.
  • A marker indicating whether you've already rated a particular tool (to prevent repeat voting).
  • An optional personal API key you enter yourself in certain tools (such as the website speed checker), if you choose to use that feature.
  • A history of recent links you've created in some tools (such as the UTM link builder), to make them easier to reuse.

This data stays on your device only and is never sent to our servers, and you can delete it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

5. Rating system data

When you rate a tool using the star-rating widget, your rating number (from 1 to 5) is sent to our server and added to the overall average displayed for that tool. We do not link this number to your name, email, or any personal identifier — it is only stored as part of an aggregate statistic (the sum and count of ratings) for each tool. Your IP address may be automatically and temporarily logged by our hosting provider as a normal part of any network request, but we do not use it for any purpose beyond preventing obvious rating-manipulation attempts.

7. Advertising & cookies

The site may display advertisements through the Google AdSense network as a way to fund running the tools for free. Google and third-party advertisers use cookies to serve ads relevant to your visits to this site and others. You can opt out of personalized advertising via Google Ads Settings, or by using ad-blocking extensions in your browser.

8. Contact form

If you reach out to us via our Contact page, we collect only your name, email address, and message content, for the sole purpose of responding to your inquiry. We do not use this data for any marketing purpose without your explicit consent, and we do not share it with any third party.

9. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data to any third party. Limited data may only be shared in the following circumstances:

  • With the third-party service providers listed in section 6, only to the extent necessary to run the tool you're using.
  • If required to do so by a binding legal order from a competent authority.
  • To protect our legal rights or the security of the site in cases of suspected clear misuse.

10. Data retention

Local storage data remains on your device until you clear it yourself. Aggregate rating data (the average and count for each tool) is kept indefinitely as part of the site's general statistics. Contact messages are kept only for as long as needed to respond to and follow up on your inquiry.

11. Your rights

At any time, you can:

  • Clear all of the site's local storage data directly through your browser's settings.
  • Request to know about or delete any personal data you've sent us via the contact form, by reaching out through our Contact page.
  • Disable advertising cookies through your browser settings or the Google Ads Settings link mentioned above.

12. Children's privacy

The site is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children in this age group. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has shared personal data with us, please contact us immediately so we can remove it.

13. Data security

We take reasonable measures to protect any data we collect from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse, including using a secure connection (HTTPS) across all pages of the site. That said, no method of transmitting data over the internet can be guaranteed to be 100% secure, and we cannot promise absolute security.

14. Links to other sites

The site may contain links to external websites (such as sources within blog articles, or services referenced within our tools). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites, and we recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any external site you visit.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. The last updated date will always be shown at the top of this page, and we recommend reviewing it periodically.

16. Contact us

For any question about this policy or your data, you can reach us via our Contact page.
