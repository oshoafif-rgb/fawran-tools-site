Contact Fawran Tools

Got a suggestion for a new tool, or ran into a technical issue? We'd love to hear from you.

Ways to reach us

General email

Reach us at hello@fawran.tools for any general question, and we'll reply within two business days.

Suggest a new tool

If you need a tool that's not on the list yet, tell us its name and what task it should accomplish, and we'll consider it for a future update.

Report a technical issue

Let us know: the tool's name, the browser you're using (Chrome, Safari, etc.), and a short description of what happened. The more detail, the faster we can fix it.

Partnerships & collaboration

For business inquiries, advertising, and partnership requests, reach us at partners@fawran.tools.

⏱️ We respond to most messages within two business days, and we're happy to reply in either English or Arabic — whichever you prefer.
