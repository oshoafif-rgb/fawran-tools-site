Why Fawran.tools?

Fawran.tools is a completely free online tools platform, built for anyone who needs a fast solution for recurring daily tasks — converting images to WebP, compressing images without losing quality, resizing an image, merging or splitting PDFs, converting images to PDF, counting words, comparing text, or generating Meta Tags and robots.txt to improve your site's SEO.

The core feature of every tool on Fawran.tools is that processing happens entirely in your browser — no need to upload your sensitive images or files to any external server, no account, and no software to install. Open the tool, do your task, and download the result.

Whether you run a blog and need a WebP image converter, need to merge PDF files for free, or manage a website and need a professional Meta Tags generator, you'll find your tool ready on Fawran.tools without the hassle.
