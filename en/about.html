About Fawran Tools

Why we built Fawran, and what makes it different from other tool websites.

Our story

Fawran Tools started from a simple observation: every time we needed to compress an image, merge a PDF, or format some text, we'd end up wading through search results full of ads, sites that demand you create an account for a task that takes two seconds, or tools that upload your files to unknown servers with zero clarity about where that data goes. We decided to build something different: an Arabic-first platform that also serves English speakers fully, bringing together the everyday tools anyone working with images, files, and text actually needs — without the complexity, and without trading away your privacy.

What we offer

Fawran Tools now includes over 100 free tools across seven main categories:

Images
Compress, convert formats, rotate, remove backgrounds
PDF
Merge, split, compress, edit pages
Developer
JSON, Base64, Regex, UUID
Text
Word count, compare, clean up

Plus SEO tools, website & blog tools, and YouTube tools — each one designed to do a single specific job as fast and simply as possible.

How the tools work

Most Fawran tools are built to run directly inside your browser using modern web technologies. That means your files — your images, documents, and text — are not uploaded to any external server during processing, and they stay on your device the whole time. The only exception is tools that, by their nature, need to communicate with an external service (like checking a website's speed, or fetching YouTube video data) — and in those cases, we make that clear directly within the tool itself. Full details are available in our Privacy Policy.

Who uses Fawran?

  • Bloggers and online store owners who need to quickly prepare web-optimized images.
  • Students and employees who work with PDF files and text on a daily basis.
  • Developers who need a quick JSON formatter or Base64 encoder without installing any software.
  • YouTube content creators looking for tools to improve titles, descriptions, and thumbnails.
  • Anyone looking for a tool that gets a specific job done without signing up or paying for a subscription.

Our values

🔒 Privacy first

Most processing happens on your device, not on our servers.

🆓 Completely free

Every tool is available for free, with no hidden usage limits or paid "pro" tier.

🚫 No sign-up

Open the tool, do your task, close the tab — no account, no email required.

🌐 Truly bilingual

Every tool and piece of content is available in both Arabic and English, at the same depth and quality.

Real ratings, not fabricated numbers

Noticed star ratings on other sites with huge numbers ("4.8 from millions of reviews") without any clarity on where they came from? On Fawran, the rating system you see under each tool is built entirely on genuine ratings from our actual visitors — if a tool hasn't been rated yet, you won't find fake stars underneath it. We believe transparency is a core part of the trust we're building with our users.

Where we're headed

We're continuously adding new tools across images, PDF files, text, SEO, and YouTube — and we regularly review existing tools to improve them and quickly fix any technical issue. If you need a specific tool that isn't available yet, or you've noticed a problem with an existing one, we'd love to hear your suggestion or feedback via our Contact page.
