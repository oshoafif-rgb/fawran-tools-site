This icon is called Favicon, which is a small detail, but It will actually affect the professionalism of your site in the eyes of the visitor. In this guide, we will explain everything about Favicon: its importance, The required sizes, and how to add it right to your site.

What is a favicon and why is it important?

A Favicon is a small icon associated with your website, appearing in many places: the browser tab, bookmarks list, mobile search results, and the icon when adding Website to the home screen. The absence of a Favicon is noticeable, giving the impression that Website is incomplete or unprofessional and making it harder for visitors to identify your tab among ten other open tabs.

The required size table and why all this number?

SizeUsage

512×512Splash screen when launching Website as an app (PWA)

The reason for multiple sizes: each device or usage context displays the icon at a different size, and using the same small file everywhere makes it appear blurry in larger contexts.

Design tips for a clear icon

Since the icon appears very small in most usages, simplicity is key. A single prominent letter or a simple symbol works much better than a complex logo with many details, because fine details disappear or turn into an unclear smudge at very small sizes (16×16). Use a background color that strongly contrasts with the text or logo color to ensure clarity even at the smallest size.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do I need more than one size for the same icon?

Every device and platform requires a different size: the browser tab needs a very small size, while the add-to-home-screen icon on a mobile requires a much larger size.

What happens if I don’t add a Favicon to my site?

The browser tab will appear with a default empty icon, giving the impression that Website is incomplete and making it harder for the visitor to identify your tab among multiple tabs.

Can a complex logo be used as a Favicon?

It is preferable to simplify the logo as much as possible, because the icon appears very small. A single prominent letter works much more clearly than a detailed logo.

Where should I place Favicon files on my site?

Usually placed in the root folder of your site, with appropriate HTML tag lines added inside the head section of each page.
