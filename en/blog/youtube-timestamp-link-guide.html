How to share a specific moment from a YouTube video with a direct link

Want to share with your friend a specific part of a long video instead of making them watch the whole thing to get to the moment you mean? YouTube supports a link that starts at an exact second. In this guide, we explain how to create that link, as well as embed code if you want to display the video from the same moment on your site.

Why is a timed link better than a regular link?

If you share a regular video link that is an hour long and you mean a specific part in the 35 minute, the person receiving the link will need They must drag the progress bar to find the intended moment, which is inconvenient in a long video. The link at the specified time opens the video and starts playing directly from the required second, so the recipient's experience It stays perfectly smooth without any extra effort.

The difference between a direct link and embed code

Direct link: opens YouTube (in the mobile app or a new browser tab) from the specified moment — suitable for sharing via WhatsApp or personal communication. Embed code: On the contrary, the video is displayed directly on your website or blog page, without the visitor leaving your page at all—very useful if you’re writing an article or guide and want to support it with a specific video segment as a reference.

How to use the tool step by step?

Practical uses

  • Educational content: Referencing a specific part of a lecture or long explanation.
  • Product review: Reference to the final recommendation part in a long review video.
  • Video Podcast: Sharing the most important moment from a long-running episode.
  • Citation in an Article: Embedding a specific video segment as a supporting reference for a point in your article.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the link at the specified time work on mobile and desktop?

Yes, the link works the same way on the YouTube mobile app, the browser on the computer, and even when shared via messaging apps.

What's the difference between a timed link and an embed code?

A timestamp link opens the video directly on YouTube, while the embed code shows the video directly on your site without leaving the page.

Can hours be specified and not just minutes in the timing?

Yes, you can specify hours, minutes, and seconds together, which is particularly useful for very long videos like podcasts.

Is using the official oEmbed better than other methods to fetch video data?

Yes, oEmbed is an official, reliable interface directly supported by YouTube, guaranteeing the accuracy of the displayed data without the need for a private API key.
