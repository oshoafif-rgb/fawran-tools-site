What are UTM links and why do you need them to track your campaigns

You shared your website link on Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp, and your visits increased — but you don't know exactly which platform brought the most visitors? UTM links are the solution. A simple link with added parameters lets analysis tools like Google Analytics know the exact source of each visit. In this guide, we will explain everything about them.

Why do you need a UTM link in the first place?

Without UTM, analytics tools only know that the visit is coming from an "external source," without precise details. If you share the same link in multiple places — a Facebook post, Instagram story, newsletter, paid ad — there's no way to distinguish their sources without UTM. With a UTM link, every version of the link carries clear information about its source, so you can see exactly which marketing channel is producing the highest actual results.

Maintaining consistency across campaigns

The biggest common mistake is inconsistency in naming values — sometimes "Facebook", sometimes "facebook", and sometimes "FB". Analytics tools treat them as completely different sources, which scatters your data and makes it hard to read. Set a consistent naming system from the start (all lowercase, no spaces) and stick to it in all your future campaigns.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the mandatory parameters to build a correct UTM link?

Three practically mandatory parameters: source, medium, and campaign. As for term and content, they are optional.

Do UTM links affect user experience or page speed?

No, parameters are just additional text at the end of the link and do not affect the page content or loading speed.

Should uppercase or lowercase letters be used in parameter values?

It is preferable to stick to a consistent case across all your campaigns, because analytics tools consider "Facebook" and "facebook" as two different sources.

Where can I see UTM link tracking results after creating them?

In "Acquisition" reports inside website analytics tools like Google Analytics.
