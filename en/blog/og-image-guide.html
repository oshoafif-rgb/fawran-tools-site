Sharing image (OG Image): Why your link shows up without an image.

Article content:

  • What is an Open Graph image?

Did you share your article link on WhatsApp or Facebook, and it appeared without an image or with a random image unrelated to the content? This problem significantly reduces the click-through rate — people are much more attracted to links with a clear image than to ordinary text links. In this guide, we will explain how to control how your link appears when shared.

What is an Open Graph image?

Open Graph is a standard used by most social platforms (Facebook, WhatsApp, LinkedIn) to display a rich preview for any link shared on them — title, description, and image. The image in particular is specified via an HTML tag called og:image In your page's code, this is the first thing that catches the viewer's attention even before they read the title.

Why does the image sometimes disappear?

Most common reasons: Tag og:image It is not present at all in the page code, the link used in the tag is incorrect or broken, or the image is inaccessible from outside your site (like images that require login). Another common reason: the platform has stored an old preview of the link (caching) and needs a manual update to display the new version.

The correct size and why it's important

The globally recommended size is 1200×630 pixelsThis size ensures that the image appears fully and clearly on most platforms without unexpected cropping of important parts. Images with completely different aspect ratios may be cropped unpredictably depending on the platform, so sticking to the standard size is the safest way to achieve consistent results everywhere.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does my article link sometimes appear without an image when shared?

Usually because the og:image tag is missing in the page code, or the link used is incorrect or inaccessible externally.

What is the correct size for an Open Graph image?

The recommended size is 1200×630 pixels, which is the ratio most platforms display correctly without unexpected cropping.

Does the same share image appear on all platforms in the same way?

Most platforms use the same Open Graph standard, but X platform uses a separate Twitter Card standard, so it is preferable to add both tags together.

How long does it take for the updated preview image to appear after editing it?

Some platforms temporarily store the link preview, so the update might take some time to appear or may require the platform's cache clearing tool.
