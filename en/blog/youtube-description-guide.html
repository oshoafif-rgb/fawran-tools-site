How to write a well-organized YouTube video description that actually serves SEO

Many content creators write the video description at the last minute, a sentence or two quickly before publishing. But a well-organized description with clear sections not only helps the viewer, it also gives you important extra space to improve your video's visibility in search. In this guide, we will explain how to write a description that serves both — the reader and the search engine.

Why is the video description more than just an explanation?

The video description is one of the most important sources YouTube relies on to understand the topic of your video in depth. A short title gives a general idea, but the description gives you much more space to explain the content in detail, include additional keywords naturally, and direct viewers to other related sources and content.

The importance of the first two visible lines

YouTube shows only about 100-125 characters of the description before cutting it off and showing a "Show More" button — meaning this part is exactly the only section viewers will see immediately without any additional click. The most important information or an attractive sentence must be placed right here, not buried in the long description that most viewers won’t reach at all.

Perfect Organized Description Structure

  • Engaging summary (first two lines): Directly answers "Why should I watch this video?"
  • Chapters (optional): The list of timings if the video is long and divided into parts.
  • Important Links: Your site, mentioned products, additional sources.
  • Social media links: Your channel accounts on other platforms.
  • Hashtags: 3-5 relevant hashtags at the end of the description.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many characters appear before "Show More" in a YouTube description?

Usually about 100-125 characters only before truncation occurs, so the most important information should be placed specifically in this range.

Is the order of sections in the description important?

It is preferable to place the attractive summary first, then the chapters if any, then important links, and finally the hashtags.

How many hashtags are appropriate in the video description?

YouTube displays only the first 3 hashtags directly above the title, and it is generally advised not to exceed 15 hashtags in the description.

Does the template retain previously used links to save time?

Yes, common links are saved locally in your browser, so you do not need to retype them every time you create a new description.
