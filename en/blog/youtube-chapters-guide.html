How to add chapters to a YouTube video the correct way

Article content:

  • Why do chapters improve the viewing experience?
  • Official terms for activating chapters
  • How to create a chapter list step by step?
  • How to write attractive chapter titles?
  • Frequently Asked Questions

Have you seen long YouTube videos with a progress bar divided into colored sections with different titles? These are "Chapters," a simple but powerful feature that lets viewers jump directly to the part that matters without watching the entire video. In this guide, we will explain how to add them correctly so that YouTube actually recognizes them."

Why do chapters improve the viewing experience?

Long videos (educational, podcasts, extended reviews) usually contain different sections; not every viewer is interested in all of them. Chapters give viewers full control — they can get a quick overview of the video's content from the chapter list and jump directly to the part that interests them. Result: a better viewing experience and higher retention rate (because the viewer isn’t forced to leave the video if the first part isn’t what they are looking for).

Official terms for activating chapters

YouTube automatically activates chapters from the description if you meet these exact conditions:

  • At least 3 chapters — Anything less than this, YouTube will not activate them.
  • The first chapter must start at 00:00 Exactly — any other time activation will be blocked.
  • Each chapter should last at least 10 seconds — Shorter chapters than this will be ignored.
  • Timings must be in ascending order — Any order mismatch will prevent activation.
  • Correct format: Time followed by a space then the chapter title, one line per chapter.

How to create a chapter list step by step?

Using Chapter and timing generator on Fawran Tools:

  1. Click "Add New Chapter" for each important part in your video.
  2. Write the timing (in minutes:seconds or hours:minutes:seconds for long videos) and a clear title for each chapter.
  3. Arrange the chapters using the "up" and "down" arrows if you need to adjust the order.
  4. Monitor the automatic verification message — the tool immediately notifies you if the first chapter does not start at 00:00 or if the number of chapters is less than 3.
  5. Click "Copy Chapters" and paste them into your YouTube video description.

How to write attractive chapter titles?

Chapter titles are not just a technical description — they are an additional opportunity to attract the viewer to continue watching. Instead of a generic title like "Part Two," write a title that clearly explains the content of that part, such as "How to Choose the Right Tool" or "Common Mistake #3." Clear and specific titles also help YouTube better understand your video's content, which relates to the overall SEO efforts of your channel.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum number of chapters YouTube requires to activate them?

YouTube requires at least 3 chapters, each chapter lasting at least 10 seconds, and the first chapter must start exactly at 00:00.

Can chapters be added after publishing the video?

Yes, you can edit the description of an already posted video and add a chapter list anytime.

Where exactly do chapters appear for the viewer?

They appear as clickable markers on the progress bar below the video, in addition to a dropdown list under the video.

Do chapters affect video ranking in search results?

Indirectly, yes — chapters improve the viewing experience and retention rate, which are two signals used by YouTube to assess video quality.
