Readability: Why short sentences improve your article

I wrote a long and detailed article, but felt that readers were leaving the page quickly? The reason might not be in the depth of the content, but in Formulation method:Long complex sentences tire the reader, even if the topic itself is very useful. In this guide, we will explain how to improve the readability of your text without losing the depth or seriousness of the content.

Why is readability important?

An online reader naturally scans the text before deciding to read it fully — text composed of large paragraphs and complex sentences gives an immediate impression of difficulty, even if the content itself is simple. The result: the reader leaves early, and the early exit rate (Bounce Rate) rises. Easy-to-read text, on the other hand, encourages the reader to continue to the end of the article, which is beneficial for user experience and for general content quality signals.

Factors Affecting Text Ease

  • Sentence Length: Sentences longer than 25-30 words are usually harder to follow and understand on the first read.
  • Word length: Complex or uncommon words slow down natural reading speed.
  • Vertebral Length: Visually large paragraphs seem tiring even before the reader actually begins.
  • Punctuation marks: Correct use of periods and commas helps the brain divide information into digestible parts.

Practical tips to simplify writing

If you find a sentence is too long, try splitting it into two sentences instead of one complex one. Use familiar everyday words instead of rare or overly formal vocabulary unless the context actually requires it. Review your paragraphs — if a paragraph is visually long (more than 4-5 lines), consider splitting it into two smaller paragraphs. Finally, read your text aloud — any sentence that makes you gasp while reading it likely needs simplification.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does readability directly affect SEO ranking?

There is no confirmed official evidence of a direct effect, but easy-to-read text increases the reader's retention rate and decreases early exit rate.

Does simplifying the text mean reducing the content depth?

No, simplification refers to the way of expression, not the depth of the ideas themselves. A complex idea can be explained with simple and clear sentences without sacrificing content.

Is the standard used in the tool official and globally recognized?

The tool uses a simple heuristic based on sentence and word length. It is not an official or validated readability standard, so use the score as guidance rather than a verdict.

What is the average ideal sentence length?

There is no strict number, but sentences exceeding 25-30 words usually become harder to follow, and it is preferable to split them.
