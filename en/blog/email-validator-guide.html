How do you make sure that the email format is correct before sending

One wrong email in a large mailing list can cause real delivery problems or make you waste time tracking an address that will never get a reply. Checking the email format before use is a simple step that saves you a lot of problems.

Most common formula mistakes

The most common mistakes when writing or copying an email address: extra space at the beginning or end of the address, forgetting the extension after the domain (like "name@example" without ".com"), using a comma instead of a period by mistake, or copying an incomplete address from a document or website.

The difference between "valid format" and "active email"

It’s very important to understand this difference: a correctly formatted address doesn’t necessarily mean it is an active email receiving messages. The address may be written correctly, but the account itself might be deleted or deactivated. Format checking only verifies the spelling, while confirming actual activity requires sending a real verification message (like an activation link by email).

Why is this important for marketing campaigns?

Sending an email campaign to a list that contains incorrectly formatted emails results in a high bounce rate, which in turn can negatively affect the sending domain's reputation with email providers, causing your future messages to go automatically to the spam folder even for correct recipients. Cleaning the list before sending is a simple preventive step that protects your reputation in the long term.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does format checking guarantee that the email is actually active and receives messages?

No, formula checking only verifies the correct spelling format. To actually confirm the email is active, you need to send a real verification message.

Are dots and dashes allowed in the username?

Yes, dots and dashes are allowed, but consecutive dots or dots at the start or end of the name are not allowed.

Does letter case affect the validity of the title?

No, case sensitivity does not affect the validity of the format, but most email providers treat it as case-sensitive.

Is it possible to check a full list of emails at once?

Yes, you can paste an entire list and get a detailed report for each title, with a total count of correct and incorrect ones.

Summary

Email format checking is a quick step but with significant value — whether you are adding a new client manually or preparing an entire mailing list, one minute of verification saves you future delivery and sending reputation problems.
