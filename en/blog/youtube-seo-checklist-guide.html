Did you post a video and forget to add chapters? Or did you miss adding important hashtags? These small mistakes accumulate and reduce the chances of your video appearing in search and suggestions. The solution: an organized checklist to review before each post to make sure you are using all the available optimization tools before the video goes public.

Why is an organized checklist better than memory?

No matter how expert you are, there is always a possibility of forgetting a single step under the pressure of time or the excitement of publishing a new video. An organized checklist turns the publishing process from relying on memory into a systematic process with guaranteed results — every important item has its place, and you review them one by one before hitting the publish button.

5 main categories covered by the list

  • Title and description: The keyword in the right place, the appropriate length, and important links.
  • Keywords and Tags: Tags actually related to the content, and appropriate hashtags.
  • Thumbnail: Custom image, clear text, contrasting colors, consistent with channel identity.
  • Inside the video: Chapters, translation, end screen, cards, and a clear call to interact.
  • Post-publishing: Appropriate playlist, pinned comment, and sharing on other platforms.

How to use the tool step by step?

Using Video SEO Checklist on Fawran Tools:

  1. Review each of the five categories and mark each item you have actually completed.
  2. Watch the live progress circle at the top of the page interact with each element you select.
  3. Use the links of the other tools integrated into the list to directly go and complete any missing item.
  4. Your progress is automatically saved in your browser, you can continue from where you left off at any time.

Which elements have top priority?

If your time is limited, focus first on the title, thumbnail, and the first two lines of the description — these three are the first things a potential viewer sees and have the biggest impact on the click decision. Other elements (chapters, cards, end screen) are important for improving viewing experience and retention rate, but they are secondary compared to the first three in terms of attracting the first click.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I apply all the menu items for each video?

It is not necessarily mandatory, but the more elements you apply, the higher the chance your video will appear better. The most important elements have top priority.

Does the order of executing items in the list matter?

It is preferable to start with the basic elements (title and keywords) before the visual design, because understanding the video topic helps you design a more accurate thumbnail.

Are post-publishing elements really important?

Yes, adding the video to a suitable playlist and sharing it on other platforms helps attract initial visits.

Does my progress in the list save when closing the browser?

Yes, your progress is saved locally in your browser automatically, so you can continue from where you left off at any later time.
