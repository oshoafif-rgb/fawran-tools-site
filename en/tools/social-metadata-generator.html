📢 Social Metadata Generator

Write your article or product topic in a sentence or two, and the tool generates an SEO title, meta description, and ready-made posts for Facebook, X, and LinkedIn, plus suggested hashtags — all in one click.

Content topic or summary

Why generate different versions per platform?

Every social platform has its own constraints and tone: X (Twitter) is capped at 280 characters, Facebook and Instagram favor an engaging tone with emoji, and LinkedIn calls for a more professional voice. The tool automatically generates a version tailored to each platform from the same topic.

Privacy

The entire generation process happens locally in your browser using fixed rules — nothing you type is ever sent to any external server.
