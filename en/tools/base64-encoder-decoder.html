Base64 Encoder & Decoder

Encode text to Base64 or decode Base64 in your browser.

Runs in your browser. No login required.

Practical notes

Base64 is transport encoding, not security encryption: anyone can decode it without a key. It is useful for embedding small data in text or moving bytes through a text-only channel, but the encoded result is roughly one third larger. Encoding and decoding happen locally, so the text you enter is not sent to a server.
