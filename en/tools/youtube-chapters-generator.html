YouTube Chapter Generator

Add timestamps and titles for important moments, then copy a correctly formatted YouTube chapter list into your video description.

YouTube requirement: The first chapter must start at 00:00, and you must have at least 3 chapters (each chapter has a duration At least 10 seconds) so YouTube enables them as navigable chapters under the video.

All processing is done locally in your browser — no need to upload any video or link

📖 Read the full guide: YouTube’s requirements for enabling chapters
