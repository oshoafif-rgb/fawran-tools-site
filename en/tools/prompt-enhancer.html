Why does a structured prompt give better results?

AI tools respond with higher quality when they know exactly: who they are (role), what the context is, what constraints apply, and what the task is precisely. An ordinary prompt often misses one or more of these elements, so the result comes back generic or missing detail.

Privacy

The entire prompt-building process happens locally in your browser using fixed rules — nothing you type is ever sent to any external server.
