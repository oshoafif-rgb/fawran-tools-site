Email List Cleaner

Paste any large email list, and strip out duplicates, invalid formats, and extra whitespace in one pass.

Practical notes

This tool removes whitespace, duplicates, and visibly invalid formats; it does not contact mail servers or guarantee deliverability. Treat the output as a preparation step before review or a trusted sending platform, and always follow consent and anti-spam rules. The list remains in your browser while it is cleaned.

Practical guide

How to use Email List Cleaner step by step

Follow these steps to get a reliable result from Email List Cleaner, checking both the inputs and output before saving or publishing.

  1. 1

    Add the inputs

    Enter the email address, list, or signature details, placing each value in its designated field.

  2. 2

    Adjust the options

    Adjust available settings such as “Paste your email list (one per line)”, “Remove invalid formats”, “Sort alphabetically” for the result you need. Start with the defaults, then refine them after previewing.

  3. 3

    Run the tool

    Run Email List Cleaner after completing the inputs, then wait for the result or analysis report to appear on the page.

  4. 4

    Review and save the result

    Review the preview or report, then use “Copy all” or “Download .csv” to save the result. Keep the original file for comparison or recovery.

Tips for the best result

Avoid confidential customer lists, and test links or signatures in a draft message before a bulk send. Core processing for this tool runs in the browser; still, keep an original copy of important files.
