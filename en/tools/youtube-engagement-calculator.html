How to read the result?

Engagement percentage = (Likes + Comments + Shares) ÷ Views x 100. As a rough general standard: Less than 1% is poor, 1% to 3% is average and normal for most channels, 3% to 6% is good, and more than 6% is excellent. These numbers are approximate and vary depending on the size of the channel and the niche — a small channel with a loyal audience may have a higher rate than a very large channel.

All calculations are done locally in your browser — your numbers are not sent to any server
