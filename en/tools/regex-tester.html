Test regular expressions and inspect matches instantly.

Practical notes

Test the expression against both matching and non-matching examples, and review flags such as g, i, and m because they change behavior. This tool uses JavaScript regular-expression syntax, which differs in places from Python or PCRE. Avoid patterns with expensive backtracking when they will process long or untrusted input.
