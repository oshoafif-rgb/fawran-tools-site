How this works

AI image tools (Midjourney, DALL-E, Stable Diffusion) respond most accurately to prompts built from clear, specific descriptors. This tool lets you compose those descriptors visually instead of memorizing prompt syntax, then joins everything into one clean, comma-separated prompt ready to paste.

Privacy

The prompt is composed entirely locally in your browser by joining your selected options — nothing is ever sent to any external server.
