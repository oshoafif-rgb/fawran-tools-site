JWT Decoder

Decode JWT headers and payloads locally.

Runs in your browser. No login required.
Practical notes
Decoding exposes a JWT header and payload for inspection, but it does not verify the signature or prove that the token is valid or trusted. Never make a security decision from decoded claims alone; verify the signature, expected algorithm, issuer, audience, and expiration on the server. Decoding here is local.
