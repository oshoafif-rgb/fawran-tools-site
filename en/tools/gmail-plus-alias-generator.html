Gmail Plus Alias Generator

Add a + tag after your username to categorize sign-ups — every address delivers straight to your main inbox.

Shopping Newsletters Social Work Banking Free trials General sign-up

How do you actually use this?

When you sign up with john+shopping@gmail.com on a shopping site, the emails still land in the same inbox as john@gmail.com. You can then set up a Gmail filter that searches for "shopping+" and files it into a separate folder or applies a label — a great way to organize your inbox without creating extra accounts.
