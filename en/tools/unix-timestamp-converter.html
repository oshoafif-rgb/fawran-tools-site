Unix Timestamp Converter

Convert Unix timestamps to dates and dates to Unix timestamps.

Runs in your browser. No login required.

Practical notes

A Unix timestamp represents an instant relative to UTC and does not carry a time zone. Converting it to local time can change the displayed hour or date according to your device settings. Also confirm whether the source value uses seconds or milliseconds; confusing them produces a wildly incorrect date.
