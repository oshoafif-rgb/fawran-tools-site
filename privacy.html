Privacy Policy

Fawran Tools is designed to process many files directly in your browser. Some tools may use third-party services when their function requires an external API. We do not ask users to create an account to use the free browser tools.

Browser processing

Many Fawran Tools utilities process files locally in the browser. In those cases, the file stays on your device unless you explicitly use a feature that communicates with an external service.

Third-party services

Some functions, such as website performance checks or video metadata retrieval, may rely on external APIs. Their own privacy policies may apply.

Storage

Favorites, recently used tools and theme preferences may be stored locally in your browser. We do not require an account for the free tools.
